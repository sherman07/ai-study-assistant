# Source Figures

- Use selected source figures, tables, equations, charts, or diagrams as evidence for understanding the source.
- Place visual-card markers such as `[[VISUAL:n]]` inside the main markdown notes near the paragraph, example, table, formula, or comparison they support.
- Use only marker IDs listed in the selected source-figure context. Do not invent marker numbers.
- Do not create a generic visual-gallery section inside the notes.
- For each selected source figure, respect its teaching intent:
  - `clarity`: place the marker where the notes first introduce or decode that slide/page concept. Keep the surrounding prose focused on making the visual clearer: what to notice, reading order, and how the figure clarifies the idea.
  - `deeper_analysis`: place the marker where the notes interpret evidence, compare cases, evaluate limits, or prepare high-quality answers. Keep the surrounding prose focused on deeper analysis of what the slide/page supports, tests, or cannot show.
- Explain the concept itself in the notes. Do not dump the visual-card captions into the markdown; the application renders the figure with its clarity/deeper-analysis explanation beside the relevant note text.
- Prefer using every selected teaching figure at least once when it materially improves understanding of the nearby slide, page, diagram, table, or example.
