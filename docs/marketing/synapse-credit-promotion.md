## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why do different actions use different numbers of credits?

Credits reflect the AI processing required to complete the work. A short question needs much less processing than analyzing a large presentation, generating detailed multi-source notes, creating an image, or supporting a live voice conversation.

### Do daily credits roll over?

No. Your plan provides a fresh allowance every day so you can maintain a consistent study routine. Your account will show the exact time of the next refresh.

### Do purchased Boost Credits expire?

Purchased Boost Credits remain in your account until you use them, subject to the account and refund terms presented when you purchase them.

### Which credits are used first?

Synapse uses your daily credits first. Any applicable promotional credits are used next, followed by purchased Boost Credits.

### What happens if a generation fails?

If Synapse cannot provide a usable result because of a platform or AI-provider failure, the reserved credits are returned automatically.

### Can an action cost more than the estimate?

Synapse shows both an expected range and a maximum charge. It will not take more purchased Boost Credits than the maximum you approved without asking for confirmation.

### Are Synapse credits money?

No. Synapse credits are usage units for eligible AI-powered services inside Synapse. They cannot be transferred, traded, withdrawn, or redeemed for cash.

### Can I continue studying after using my daily credits?

Yes. You can use purchased Boost Credits to continue immediately, or wait until your fresh daily allowance becomes available.
